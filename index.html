How to use

Enter your current level, amount of players, party size, difficulty, and amount of golden gifts (and if you have the Nothing? curse that affects pricing).

On the "Owned Upgrades" page, click on all the upgrades you currently own, which will update the shop section on the bottom to match your shop in game.

Click on items in the shop section to preview purchasing them, and items become red if you can no longer afford them.
